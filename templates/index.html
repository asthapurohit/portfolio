About

I'm a BTech CSE graduate from Parul University (Apr 2025) who thinks in systems. I'm drawn to the gap between what users intend to do and what they actually do — and I work backwards from behavior to find out why products fail them.

My work lives at the intersection of behavioral economics and product design. I write PRDs to practice structured thinking, run teardowns to train my eye, and build data tools to stay close to signals.

The PM I'm becoming: one who treats user behavior as the spec, not the variable.

Interests: Behavioral economics, habit formation, 0→1 products
